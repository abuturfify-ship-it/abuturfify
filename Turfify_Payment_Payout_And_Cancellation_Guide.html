Architectural Pillars of the Turfify Payment Engine

1. Automated Marketplace Splits (Razorpay Route)

Zero manual banking transfers. The moment a ₹1,500 booking is authorized, Razorpay Route programmatically carves 90% (₹1,335 net after 1% TDS) to the turf owner's linked bank account and retains 10% (₹150) in Turfify's platform treasury.

2. Zero-Balance Negative Offset Mechanism

If a refund is authorized when the merchant balance is ₹0, Razorpay automatically creates a negative merchant balance (e.g. -₹1,500) and reconciles it against incoming weekend transactions automatically, eliminating manual replenishment.

3. Time-Decayed 4-Tier Cancellation Matrix

Protects turf owners from catastrophic last-minute slot abandonment. Cancellations made within 6 hours of kickoff are strictly non-refundable (0%), ensuring owners keep their earnings even if funds have already settled.

4. In-App Wallet Credit Optimization (₹0 Gateway Cost)

Encourages players to take refunds as 100% Turfify Wallet credits. Cash never leaves the platform, zero payment gateway fees are burned, and 100% of customer spending remains locked in the Turfify sports ecosystem.

Section 2: Zero-Balance Gateway Mechanics

The Saturday Zero-Balance Problem: How Razorpay Handles It

When an authorized cancellation occurs and your Razorpay gateway balance is strictly ₹0.00 (because the payout already settled into your bank on Friday), here is the exact technical execution path.

RAIL A: AUTOMATIC OFFSET
Negative Merchant Balance

Razorpay permits the refund to proceed even with ₹0 funds by driving the account balance into the negative (e.g., -₹1,500.00).

Auto-Recovery: Upcoming bookings on Saturday afternoon automatically replenish this deficit. Zero penalties, no manual bank transfers needed.
RAIL B: QUEUED STATE
Queued / Pending Refund

If the merchant account has reached its negative balance tolerance limit, Razorpay places the refund in an active "Queued" queue.

Execution: The very instant the next customer pays for a turf slot, Razorpay intercepts the incoming cash flow and clears the queued refund.
RAIL C: MANUAL PRE-FUND
Instant Dashboard Top-Up

If there are no anticipated weekend transactions, the merchant can instantly top up their Razorpay balance directly via NetBanking, UPI, or NEFT.

Operation: Navigate to Razorpay Dashboard → Balance → Add Funds. Funds reflect instantly to satisfy pending refunds.

Reversing the Turf Owner's Split via Razorpay Route

When an eligible refund occurs, the platform must never bear the turf owner's portion. Razorpay Route provides the reverse_all: 1 API instruction to automatically claw back the transfer: 

// Invoking an Idempotent Refund with Automated Route Transfer Reversal
const refund = await razorpay.payments.refund(paymentId, {
  amount: refundAmountInPaise,      // Full or partial refund amount
  reverse_all: 1,                    // Automatically reverses the turf owner's 90% transfer!
  notes: { booking_id: "TRF_BK_8921", reason: "Customer initiated cancellation" }
});
CRUCIAL BUSINESS REALITY Should a Saturday Cancellation Even Get a 100% Refund?

NO! A customer who books on Wednesday for Saturday has blocked prime venue inventory for 3 days.

If they cancel on Saturday morning (< 6 hours before kickoff), standard industry practice and Turfify's Master Policy classify this as Strictly Non-Refundable (0% Refund). The turf owner keeps their 85% payout because the slot cannot be re-booked on short notice. Therefore, the zero-balance refund issue is prevented by business logic!

// 1. Wednesday: Create Route Order with Settlement Held Until Saturday Match
const order = await razorpay.orders.create({
