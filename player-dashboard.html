Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I cancel a match reservation? Click "Cancel" on any upcoming booking. A secure 6-digit confirmation code will be dispatched to your Gmail. Entering the OTP immediately confirms the cancellation and updates slot availability.
❓ How do refunds work for online bookings? Online payments made via UPI or Card are automatically queued for refund upon cancellation and settle back to your source account within 2-4 business days.
❓ What happens if it rains heavily on an outdoor turf? In the event of unplayable waterlogging, the facility owner will either reschedule your match or issue a full credit refund to your account.
